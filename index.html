Why this matters

Local businesses need software they can use on day one.

Most small businesses do not need a one-time technology project. They need a reliable platform that turns their business details into a working online presence, captures demand, and keeps leads warm until the owner can respond.

Onboarding

From business profile to deployed platform instance.

Dreamsite's onboarding flow turns a few business inputs into a configured presence layer: site structure, lead paths, search basics, analytics, and AI intake rules.

  1. Profile

    The owner adds offerings, location, proof, photos, timings, and the main customer action.

  2. Generate

    The platform generates the site map, offer pages, local SEO structure, FAQs, and AI intake scope.

  3. Configure

    Dreamsite configures the business instance with lead capture, WhatsApp routing, booking links, and analytics.

  4. Deploy

    The system deploys the web presence, validates contact paths, and keeps the setup ready for future automations.

AI layer

Dreamsite's AI layer works like a practical front desk.

Our intake engine answers common questions, collects the details an owner needs, and converts messy inquiries into structured lead records. The platform's lead system can prepare the next action without forcing the business to manage another dashboard. Voice input, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech are part of the product direction for businesses where calls and WhatsApp voice notes are still the natural interface.

Agentic AI

Reusable agents deployed from Dreamsite's platform.

Dreamsite's agentic layer is built as reusable platform infrastructure inspired by OpenCLAW-style task execution and AG-UI-style agent-to-interface patterns. Agents are configured for a business category, then deployed across multiple customers with the same safety model, approval flow, and logging foundation.

01

Tool-using workflows

Agents can operate across forms, email, calendars, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and website content through controlled platform connectors.

02

Owner-in-the-loop control

High-impact actions are drafted, reviewed, approved, and logged so automation helps the owner without taking over the business.

03

Reusable local automations

Lead follow-up, inquiry summaries, urgency routing, FAQ refreshes, and content suggestions are deployed as shared Dreamsite modules.

04

Category playbooks

Clinics, salons, restaurants, tutors, repair shops, and stores can run category-specific workflows from the same AI infrastructure.

05

Agent-to-UI experiences

The roadmap includes dynamic interfaces where agents can show forms, status, choices, and next steps instead of sending only plain chat replies.

06

Voice-first intake

Speech-to-text and text-to-speech modules are planned for owners and customers who prefer calls, voice notes, and spoken follow-up.
